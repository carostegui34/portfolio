Sushi Crawl in Buenos Aires

Influencer event · Masters of Sushi · El Gourmet · 2025

20 creators and journalists, 4 sushi restaurants, one afternoon

In 2025, to promote the new series Masters of Sushi, which highlighted the best sushi places in Buenos Aires, we approached the go-to-market strategy from a retention and awareness perspective. The team produced thousands of branded napkins and chopsticks, distributed across the 15 restaurants featured in the show.

To build relationships with influencers, we produced a Sushi Crawl hosted by the show's lead: an afternoon taking 20 content creators and journalists through four sushi restaurants. We chose creators who cover not only food but also lifestyle and weekend plans in the city. Half were long-time partners and half were new recruits.

I handled the event's pre-production, coordinated the group on the ground from one restaurant to the next, and coordinated the social media coverage with the filmmakers on site.

Give Me Back My Tupper

Reactive creator partnership · El Gourmet · 2024

Engagement rate 100% above benchmark

How to act fast when something goes viral. Our new show Give Me Back My Tupper was about a classic complaint from parents: after every family gathering, a food container goes missing from the kitchen. Just two months before the premiere, a video of a daughter bringing her mother a huge bag full of Tupperware went viral nationwide.

We had to move fast. I contacted the creator and arranged to send her mom a big bag of new containers with a note signed by her favorite chef from our brand. The video she posted beat our engagement benchmark by 100%.

It wasn't part of the planned go-to-market strategy, but it served its goals: brand awareness and fan retention.

Interview with the Vampire

Exclusive avant-premiere · AMC · 2024

40+ content creators, most of them new to the brand

I took part in producing the launch event for one of AMC's most anticipated series: an exclusive avant-premiere of Interview with the Vampire.

More than 40 content creators attended, and most had no previous relationship with the brand. The team chose them not only for their reach but for their personal passion for this kind of story.

My role covered venue hunting, vendor coordination and the social media coverage of the event.
