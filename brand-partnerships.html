Brand Partnerships

Partnerships that traded quality content for reach, and product placement built into our recipes.

Pinterest

Always-on partnership · Más Chic, AMC Networks LATAM · 2024

Free advertising and a spot on Pinterest's homepage weekly picks

In 2024, AMC Networks' lifestyle brands, including Más Chic, had an always-on partnership with Pinterest: we delivered content in large quantities in exchange for extra reach and promotion from the platform.

For the show Locas por el Jean, the social media team created more than 20 pieces, images and video, on the history of denim. In return, Más Chic got free advertising and a place in the weekly curated list of recommendations on Pinterest's homepage.

Oster

Product placement · A Pura Dulzura · El Gourmet, AMC Networks LATAM

Two sponsored recipes, a website banner and a newsletter sponsorship

With more than 1 million followers on Instagram, El Gourmet had a reputation that made product placement a natural fit, and it was something I always pursued together with the sales team.

For the campaign A Pura Dulzura, the kitchen appliance brand Oster provided products featured in two recipes for social media. The promotional package also included a banner on the website and a sponsorship in the newsletter for subscribers.
