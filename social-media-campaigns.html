Social Media Campaigns

Social campaigns that launched and sustained El Gourmet shows, built from recipes, chefs and the stories around the food.

Medialuna's Masters

Show campaign · Maestros de la Medialuna · El Gourmet · 2025

14K likes and 200+ comments on a single recipe reel

To promote Maestros de la Medialuna, which aired every Wednesday across Argentina, Mexico, Colombia, Peru and Chile, we turned chef Juan Manuel Herrera's recipes into short reels: butter medialunas one week, lard medialunas the next. Each reel gave the full recipe and closed with the show's schedule, so a useful post doubled as a tune-in reminder.

The Construction Barbecue

Show launch · Asado de Obra · El Gourmet · 2025

37K likes and 1,000+ comments on a single reel

Asado de Obra follows an Argentine tradition: construction workers grilling meat at the work site. Around its April 2 premiere, the campaign put the people behind the grill at the center, from Pedro sharing his tips for lighting the fire to a reel on the camaraderie, patience and seasoning that make a construction barbecue special.

The launch also included Women in the Barbecue, which told the story from a female construction worker's point of view and reached 150% above our engagement benchmark.

  • Camaraderie at the grill

Pizza Masters

Show campaign · Maestros de la Pizza · El Gourmet · 2023

35K likes on a single recipe reel

For Maestros de la Pizza, which aired Tuesdays and Thursdays, the campaign mixed visits to real pizzerias, like a fugazzeta reel shot at Pizzería La Mezzetta, with recipes from chef Roberto Petersen. The fugazzeta reel alone drew 23K likes and 470+ comments.
