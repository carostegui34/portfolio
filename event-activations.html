Brand Marketing · AMC Networks Latin America · 2025

Event Activations

Booths at two of Buenos Aires' biggest events, each built around a clear goal: new subscribers, or brand awareness and relationships with creators.

To decide which actions deserved budget, I worked with the management team on a checklist every marketing action had to meet: reach, low effort vs. high impact, team building and prestige.

Caminos y Sabores

Brand partnership · El Gourmet, AMC Networks Latin America · 2025

5,000+ booth visitors, each one a new newsletter subscriber

El Gourmet, AMC Networks Latin America's food network, turned 25 in 2025. To announce the next month's show schedule, we partnered with Caminos y Sabores, one of the biggest food conventions in Buenos Aires.

The branding team built a booth with games and experiences for fans, and I produced four cooking masterclasses with chefs from our talent pool. That included negotiating budgets with talent, on-stage production, running the meet-and-greets and the social media coverage.

More than 5,000 people visited our booth. Signing up for our newsletter was the condition to take part, so each visit became a new subscriber.

Buenos Aires Comic Con

Booth activation · AMC · Dead City season 2 · 2025

Every invited creator joined AMC's close friends list

In 2025, AMC took part in Buenos Aires Comic Con to promote season 2 of Dead City. There was no partnership this time: the team paid for a booth, with brand awareness and relationships with content creators as the goals.

Every content creator invited to the booth got a gift and was immediately added to AMC's close friends list on social media, where we published exclusive news and invitations. The activation also met one of our internal success metrics, prestige, since this is one of the biggest fan conventions in Latin America.

I worked on the booth's pre-production and the social media coverage of the event.
