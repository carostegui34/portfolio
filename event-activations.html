Brand Marketing · El Gourmet, AMC Networks LATAM · 2023 & 2025

Event Activations

5,000+ booth visitors, each one a new newsletter subscriber

I produced and executed brand partnerships at Caminos y Sabores, a food festival, in 2023 and at Buenos Aires Comic Con in 2025. With both organizers I negotiated sponsorships that included booths at the venue, marketing and press integrations, and social media collaborations. I also negotiated fees with chefs and talent to engage with fans at the events, and with the vendors who built the booth and covered it for social media.

To decide which actions deserved budget, I worked with the management team on a checklist every marketing action had to meet: reach, low effort vs. high impact, team building and prestige.

More than 5,000 people visited our booth at Caminos y Sabores, and signing up for our newsletter was the condition to take part, so each visit became a new subscriber. Comic Con 2025 delivered the same result.
