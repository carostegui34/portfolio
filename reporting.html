Video feature · Playbill

The Truck Where It Happens

The story of Jeffrey, a young Latino New Yorker who delivers Playbills to Broadway theaters every day. I found it walking through the Theatre District, pitched it to the outlet and to Jeffrey, and filmed over two days at the Queens warehouse and on his truck. I researched, produced, shot and edited it.
